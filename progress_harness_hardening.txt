Design note (in depth, not breadth): findings() takes the interpreter as a
parameter (default sys.executable). main() resolves venv_python(root, os.name),
exits 2 if it is missing, and passes it in. If findings() required root/.venv
itself, CI (pip install into the runner's python, no .venv) and every
mutation copy (.venv skipped) would fail. The no-venv exit applies to .py
edits only; other edits stay silent, as before.
